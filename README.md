# Corn Rush

A swipe-to-sort corn game for your phone. Keep the good corn, toss the yuck, chase the rare cobs.

**Play:** invite only for now. Ask for an invite link.

## Add it to your Home Screen

- **iPhone:** open your invite link in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open your invite link in Chrome, tap the menu, then **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).

It then opens full screen like an app and works offline.

## How to play

- Swipe right (or tap Keep) for good corn. Swipe left (or tap Toss) for mouldy corn. Deeper into a run, watch for sneaky rot that looks almost fine.
- A wrong swipe or a timeout costs a life. Three strikes and the harvest is over.
- Streaks multiply your points: x2 at 5 in a row, x3 at 10, and fast swipes score +1.
- Rare cobs: Golden +5, Rainbow +10, Galaxy +15, Diamond +25. A Life cob gives a life back.
- Rarer tiers unlock with lifetime points: Lava +40 (4,000), Ghost +60 (12,000), Royal +100 (30,000), Uni-corn +200 (75,000).
- Every 4,000 lifetime points, the next cob is a guaranteed rare. The Cornpedia tracks every type you've caught.
- Fill the Frenzy bar for 6 seconds of all-good corn at double points.
- Every point is a kernel. Spend kernels on backgrounds; hit milestones to unlock cob skins.
- Three new missions every day, plus a daily streak bonus.
- Extremely rare cobs unlock much later: Candy +250, Neon +300, Alien +400, Void +500 and Angel +1,000.
- The shop has four tabs: scenes (12 backgrounds), cob skins (9), husk colours (6) and camos (8, earned through challenges).
- The home screen plays an original theme tune. The speaker button mutes everything.
- On your last life you can revive once for 250 kernels. The leaderboard keeps each player name's single best run on that phone.

Progress is saved on your own device.

## Files
