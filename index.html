How to play

Swipe right to keep good corn. Swipe left to toss the yuck.

Rare cobs are worth big points. Rarer tiers unlock as you play

Streaks multiply points: x2 at 5 in a row, x3 at 10. Fast swipes score +1. Fill the FRENZY bar for a x2 all-good-corn rush. Every point becomes a kernel to spend on skins or a second chance.
